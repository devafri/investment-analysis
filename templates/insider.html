{# Methodology note #}
Methodology
An insider is Routine if they traded in the same calendar month in ≥3 unique years. All other trades are Opportunistic. Research shows opportunistic trades predict future stock returns; routine trades do not.

Cohen, Malloy & Pomorski (2010)
