{# TTM info #}
For trailing-twelve-month (TTM) figures, ingest enough consecutive quarters to include: the most recent 10-K, the most recent 10-Q after it, and a 10-Q from one fiscal year before that (same fiscal quarter) — typically 4–5 quarterly ZIPs. Companies with insufficient history will show which piece is missing on their detail page rather than a wrong number.
Insider trading data is auto-detected from data/insider_trading (or the same directory as quarterly data). Place SEC EDGAR Form 3/4/5 ZIP files (containing NONDERIV_TRANS.txt, SUBMISSION.txt, REPORTINGOWNER.txt) there.
